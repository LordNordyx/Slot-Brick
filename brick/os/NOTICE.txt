Slot OS: what else is on this card, where it comes from, and under what licence
==============================================================================

Slot OS starts the TrimUI Brick from this card straight into Slot. Slot itself, and Slot
OS's own scripts (init, mixer.sh, the image build), are GPL-3.0: see
Slot/System/licenses/slot-GPL-3.0.txt. Everything else listed here is someone else's work,
passed on unchanged.

TrimUI's boot chain and kernel
------------------------------
boot0, the u-boot boot package (u-boot, the secure monitor, the SCP firmware, the device
tree) and the Linux 4.9.191 kernel in the boot partition are TrimUI's own, from their
firmware 1.1.1 (26 November 2025), what the Brick's stock system runs:
  https://github.com/trimui/firmware_brick/releases/tag/v1.1.1-20251126
Linux and u-boot are GPL-2.0 (GPL-2.0.txt). TrimUI has not published their source; it is
theirs to provide.
The u-boot settings (env) and the card's layout, which let the Brick start from an SD card,
are Knulli's (https://github.com/knulli-cfw/knulli-linux), with the kernel's console
messages turned down.

Kernel modules, firmware and GPU libraries
------------------------------------------
From the same TrimUI firmware, as their system has them:
- pvrsrvkm.ko, dc_sunxi.ko: the PowerVR GE8300 GPU's kernel driver, by Imagination
  Technologies, Dual MIT/GPL. Debug sections removed.
- rgx.fw.22.102.54.38, rgx.sh.22.102.54.38: the GPU's firmware, Imagination Technologies.
- libEGL, libGLESv2, libIMGegl, libglslcompiler, libpvrNULL_WSEGL, libsrv_um, libusc and
  pvrsrvctl: Imagination Technologies' PowerVR GE8300 driver (DDK 1.19).
- xradio_mac.ko, xradio_core.ko, xradio_wlan.ko: the XR829 Wi-Fi chip's driver, by
  XRadioTech, GPL. Debug sections removed.
- boot_xr829.bin, fw_xr829.bin, sdd_xr829.bin, etf_xr829.bin: the XR829's firmware.

Debian packages
---------------
glibc, libstdc++, ALSA, busybox, wpa_supplicant, the FAT and partition tools and the
libraries they use are Debian 12 (bookworm) arm64 packages. Each package's copyright and
licence file is in debian/, and debian/packages.txt lists the exact versions. Their
source, at those versions: https://snapshot.debian.org and https://sources.debian.org.

Thanks to the Knulli team, whose work on the Brick made Slot OS possible.
